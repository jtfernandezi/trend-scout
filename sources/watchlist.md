# Watchlist

Where to look for signals each run. The goal is **venture-scale, AI-native**
ideas, so lead with frontier-capability and big-market signals. Still cross one
AI capability with one large structural shift — but the structural half must
point at a big market, not a niche.

## AI / frontier-capability signals (lead here)
- New model/lab releases and capability jumps (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google
  DeepMind, Meta, Mistral) — what's newly *possible* this month
- Y Combinator Requests for Startups (current edition) + recent batch companies
- a16z / Sequoia / Bessemer / Greylock published theses and "what we want to fund"
- Hacker News front page + Show HN (last 7 days) — what builders are shipping
- Product Hunt top launches (last 7 days)
- AI research → product: agent frameworks, eval/observability, inference infra,
  memory, retrieval, tool-use, voice, multimodal, on-device

## High-impact domains to mine (all in scope)
- **AI infra & agents** — the picks-and-shovels layer: orchestration, evals,
  inference, memory, agent security/governance, dev tooling for AI
- **Applied AI in big verticals** — AI rebuilding large labor-budget industries:
  healthcare, legal, financial services, defense/gov, insurance, biotech, science
- **Frontier / deep tech** — robotics, bio×AI, energy, hardware, materials,
  scientific discovery (capital-intensive, high-impact, YC hard-tech tilt)
- **Consumer & prosumer AI** — AI-native consumer/prosumer products with
  viral/high-growth potential and large TAM

## Large structural shifts (the non-AI half of the cross)
- Platform shifts and new distribution surfaces (new app stores, protocols, agents-as-users)
- Big labor/spend budgets moving (the 13% of GDP that is labor, not the 1% that is IT)
- Demographic and regulatory waves — but only where they unlock a *large* market
- M&A / PE / capital-formation patterns at scale

## Verification sources (for the adversarial novelty check)
- YC company directory (ycombinator.com/companies)
- Crunchbase / general "[idea] startup" + recent funding searches
- Product Hunt / GitHub search for the specific capability or workflow
