# Watchlist

Where to look for signals each run. Mix AI-capability sources with non-AI
structural-trend sources — the prompt requires crossing one of each.

## AI / tech capability signals
- Y Combinator Requests for Startups (current edition)
- Hacker News front page (last 7 days)
- Product Hunt top launches (last 7 days)
- Major model/lab releases (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind)
- a16z / Sequoia / Bessemer published theses

## Non-AI structural trend signals
- Demographic shifts (retirement waves, migration, household formation)
- Regulatory changes (state/federal, by industry)
- Labor market shortages/surpluses by sector
- Industry-specific trade press (construction, healthcare, logistics, etc.)
- M&A / PE activity patterns (roll-ups, take-privates, search funds)

## Verification sources (for the adversarial novelty check)
- YC company directory (ycombinator.com/companies)
- Crunchbase / general "[idea] startup" search
- Product Hunt search for the specific workflow
