## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Path Robotics**: **300M dollar Series E at about 2.45bn dollars** led by Georgian (2026-11-10/11), with
   company-reported **ARR up about 17x in 18 months**. Customers include Huntington Ingalls and Lockheed Martin.
   **Mechanism:** a learned vision and control model lets welding robots handle part-to-part variation, so automation
   pays off in high-mix, low-volume fabrication. **Not re-pointed:** welding flanks were already killed at ts-0237,
   ts-0370 and ts-0482.
2. **Blue J**: **176M dollar Series E led by JMI Equity**, valuation above 700M dollars, with **6,500+ organisations**
   (up from about 1,000 two years ago) and company-reported **ARR growth of 165% over about 15 months**.
   **Mechanism:** cited answers to hard tax questions across the whole body of tax law, now expanding into return
   review and responses to government notices.
3. **Daylight Security**: **80M dollar Series B led by Goldman Sachs Alternatives**, with a reported valuation of
   about 400M dollars and **revenue up more than 40x in a year**. Customers include Lovable, LastPass, Miro and
   dLocal. **Mechanism:** AI agents investigate security alerts and human specialists escalate, so managed
   detection and response (MDR) runs at close to software margins.
4. **Charta Health**: **55M dollar Series B led by Insight Partners** (about 85M dollars raised in total). Customers
   include American Family Care, Grow Therapy and Pediatric Associates, and it reports double-digit cuts in denial
   rates. **Mechanism:** before a claim goes out, AI checks the medical record against coding rules and each insurer's
   policies.
5. **Oura acquiring Counsel Health for 625M dollars** (TechCrunch, 2026-11-10). **Mechanism:** an AI doctor with
   licensed-physician oversight delivers primary care at near-zero marginal cost. It was used as new evidence for
   ts-0381, not as a new idea.
6. **Abby Care**: **82M dollar Series C** led by Transformation Capital with CVS Health Ventures (Fierce tracker,
   2026-10-15). It operates in eight states, reported about 15M dollars of 2025 revenue, and members had 40%+ fewer
   hospitalisations. **Mechanism:** AI-supported training and compliance turn family caregivers into certified,
   Medicaid-reimbursed workers.
7. **Rox**: **100M dollar Series B at 1.3bn dollars** (General Catalyst, March Capital). ARR is reportedly up more
   than tenfold in its first year, with net revenue retention above 700% (press reports, unconfirmed by a primary
   source). **Mechanism:** agents run the sales team's account research and pipeline work on top of the CRM.
   **Not re-pointed:** sales agents are crowded, and every regulated variant runs into the CRM record holders
   (Salesforce Financial Services Cloud, Wealthbox, Redtail).
8. **Mount Thor** (45M dollars across seed and Series A, CRV and Conviction) and **Inkbox** (5.3M dollar seed, 300K
   dollars ARR four months after YC). **Mechanism:** computer-use agents need fleets of real desktops, not just
   browsers.

**Patterns worked and whether they are exhausted:**
- **Blue J: three attempts, all closed.** Each one died on the party that already holds the taxpayer's data or the
  law: the Indian content publisher, the crypto-tax apps, and the existing resolution firms, which have no software
  revenue to protect. The channel conflict was real but it only blocks Blue J. **Lesson: a channel conflict
  only opens a segment if no one else is already selling the outcome to that buyer.**
- **Charta: two attempts, both closed, and the closing pattern is new.** Software vendors in revenue cycle and
  compliance are now **bearing risk themselves**. Brellium pays clawbacks it should have caught, Klaim and Claritev
  promise certainty of payment, and Rivio (2026-11-07) guarantees hospital revenue. The insurer-of-record move that
  produced ts-0586 and ts-0594 is already standard practice in healthcare revenue. **Treat healthcare
  revenue cycle as exhausted for insurer-of-record ideas.**
- **Daylight: one attempt, closed.** European MDR is crowded, and Eye Security already bundles MDR with insurance.
  The AI-plus-human managed-security pattern has local copies in every major region checked.
- **Abby Care and Mount Thor: one attempt each, closed** by fast followers and by sandbox infrastructure already in
  the ledger.

## Method note for the next run

**One. Add a new pre-check: "is the vertical's software already bearing risk?"** In healthcare revenue cycle the
software vendors now guarantee outcomes themselves (Brellium, Klaim and Claritev, Rivio). Before proposing an
insurer-of-record idea, search "[vertical] guarantee" and "[vertical] clawback" along with the leaders' names. It
costs one search and would have saved two candidates this run.

**Two. A channel conflict needs a second check.** A channel conflict blocks the named leader, but the segment is
only open if no services firm or record holder already sells that outcome to the buyer. Name the second-best
occupant before writing the candidate down.

**Three. Leave the Fall 2026 sweep until Demo Day (2026-12-02).** Pre-Demo Day Fall 2026 companies have published no
revenue. Use the next run for the Readily/Suki healthcare-compliance pattern and any new raises with hard numbers.

**Four. Skip for now:** Blue J, Charta, Daylight, Abby Care, Mount Thor and Inkbox, Rox, Path Robotics (welding), and
everything on the 2026-11-10 skip list. Still open: ts-0594's SDI broker rate and Procore API terms, and ts-0381's
RFP-scoring check.
