**Run type:** scheduled research pass (sixty-fourth run under the venture-scale-gated thesis).
**Outcome:** **Zero surviving ideas. Nine new kills (ts-0595 to ts-0603).** No brief this run. ts-0594 is re-checked
and stays `new`. No other held-book entry changes status.

How this run followed the 2026-11-07 method note:

- **Note one (insurer-of-record needs a size gate) was the main move. It killed three ideas and produced no
  survivor.** Three size-gated insurance products were tried: R&W cover for small deals (ts-0599), full-limit supplier
  business-interruption cover (ts-0600) and product liability under the new EU directive (ts-0598). In both cases
  where the gate was explicit, specialists had already lowered it. CFC's TLPE serves sub-30M dollar deals with no
  underwriting fee, and Marsh's Sentrisk already maps suppliers for CBI placement. **The size-gate list is now mostly
  worked.** Captives (ts-0344, ts-0388) and trade credit were not re-opened. Trade credit has visible SME products
  (Allianz Trade SME, Credable), so it was not given its own entry.
- **Note two (verify ts-0594's weakest facts) was half done.** The size-floor fact is re-confirmed: there is still no
  lower-threshold SDI programme. Hudson's floor is 75M dollars of revenue, Arch requires 150M dollars of revenue plus
  100M dollars of annual enrolment, and AXA XL requires 100M dollars+ of subcontract cost. The broker rate and Procore
  API checks need a human conversation and remain open.
- **Note three (skip list) was respected.** Kalepa, Clark and Zipline appear only as pattern or context evidence.

## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Sumary** (Copenhagen → London): **25M dollar seed led by Singular, 2026-11-10**, which it calls the largest
   accounting-software seed in Europe, less than a year after a 4.2M dollar pre-seed. It has contracts with
   **Deloitte, Grant Thornton and Baker Tilly** and has signed **most of the 20 largest Nordic accounting firms**.
   It reports 50% faster monthly closes and 75% faster VAT returns (company-reported). **Mechanism:** agents do an
   accounting firm's recurring compliance work (close, VAT, year-end) inside the client's own ledger, and the
   accountant approves it.
2. **Finite State**: **27M dollar Series C led by The O.H.I.O. Fund**, with National Grid Partners, Energy Impact
   Partners and CIBC, 2026-11-10. Customers include national power grids, a US national-security lab, global
   automakers and medical-device makers. No revenue was disclosed. **Mechanism:** binary analysis of the firmware inside a
   finished device finds exploitable vulnerabilities and produces signed CRA, FDA and UNECE R155 compliance packages
   without source code.
3. **Nominal**: **240M dollar Series C led by Lightspeed and Insight** (Fortune Term Sheet, 2026-11-10), eight months
   after an 80M dollar B-2 at a 1bn dollar valuation. Reportedly **four of the five largest US defence primes** are
   customers. **Mechanism:** one data layer over every test stand and telemetry stream in hardware development, with AI
   that reads each test run.
4. **Alterra Labs**: **12.5M dollar seed led by Oak HC/FT, 2026-11-10**. It has revenue from training environments
   and post-training work, and **frontier labs are its fastest-growing customers**. **Mechanism:** clinicians design
   medical tasks and graders that become evaluation and reinforcement-learning environments for frontier models.
5. **Guava** (formerly Gridspace): **12.5M dollar seed led by Acrew, 2026-11-06**. It reports **10B+ live agent
   minutes and 100+ deployments** (company-reported). **Mechanism:** a voice-agent API with its own speech stack for
   healthcare, financial-services and insurance calls. **Not re-pointed:** the voice flanks were worked at ts-0474 and
   ts-0477.
6. **Onodrim Industries**: a **reported 300M dollar Series A at 1.3bn dollars** (Tech Startups roundup, 2026-11-09;
   September reports said a ~200M euro round was in talks with Lux). It follows a 40M euro seed in February.
   **Mechanism:** buy existing European defence component suppliers and modernise their throughput. It is used only
   as context for ts-0603, because the roll-up itself fails the AI-native gate.

Also scanned and not re-pointed:

**Patterns worked and whether they are exhausted:**
- **Sumary: two attempts, both closed.** Both of the obvious flanks were already held. Running the firm yourself is
  done by Multiplier, Cortea and others. Taking the software abroad runs into the local system-of-record vendors,
  which shipped their agents first: TKC and freee in Japan, DATEV in Germany (ts-0383). Accounting-firm automation
  is now **exhausted in every major market the ledger has tried** (US, UK, Germany, Japan, Nordics).
- **Finite State: two attempts, both closed.** The importer flank had already been killed at ts-0389, and ONEKEY
  occupies it. The insurer flank fails because the tech MGAs can adopt scans the same way cyber insurers did.
- **Nominal: two attempts, both closed by the system-of-record rule.** For regulated or foreign hardware buyers, the
  data already sits with MES vendors (pharma) or test-equipment makers (Siemens, HBK, dSPACE), and they are adding
  AI.
- **Size-gated insurance: three attempts, all closed.** The R&W and CBI gates have already been lowered by specialist
  MGAs and brokers. **The gate is only an opening where it is enforced by a regulator or by a large incumbent's
  channel economics, as with SDI's surety cannibalisation.** Where the gate is just a cost floor, specialists like CFC
  arrive first.

## Method note for the next run

**One. Stop mining two areas: accounting-firm automation and size-gated insurance where the gate is only a cost
floor.** Re-open them only with a structural block that has not been tried before.

**Two. Fall 2026 is the next high-density source.** YC Fall 2026 Demo Day is 2026-12-02. In the runs before it, look
for Fall 2026 companies that have publicly reported revenue and for Spring 2026 breakouts that are raising Series A
rounds. This week's funding news was thin on disclosed traction. Four of the six breakouts gave no revenue figure.

**Three. Apply the system-of-record rule before writing a candidate down.** It killed three of nine this run (TKC
and freee, the MES vendors, Siemens and HBK). Name the vertical's record-holder and check its last two quarters of
releases first. That costs one search per candidate, versus four for a full kill check.

**Four. Skip for now:** Sumary, Finite State, Nominal, Alterra, Guava and Onodrim, plus everything on the
2026-11-07 skip list. Still open for ts-0594: the real SDI rate from a construction broker, and Procore's API terms.
