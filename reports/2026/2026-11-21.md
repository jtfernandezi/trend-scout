## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Applied Compute**: **Palo Alto Networks is buying it for 4.5bn dollars in stock** (2026-11-18, 8-K). It reached a
   **100M dollar annualised revenue run rate in under 18 months**, 16 days after raising 350M dollars at 3.25bn
   dollars. **Mechanism:** post-train and serve open-weight models on a company's own data.
2. **Skydio**: **Axon is putting in 1.2bn dollars more at 5.2bn dollars pre-money**, taking about 37%, and becomes
   Skydio's exclusive US and Canada public-safety distributor (2026-11-05). Skydio expects **about 1bn dollars of
   bookings this fiscal year.** **Mechanism:** autonomous docked drones fly to an incident before people arrive and
   stream it to a remote operator.
3. **Corma**: **125M dollar Series A led by NEA at 1bn dollars** (TechCrunch, 2026-11-16), three months after a 60M
   dollar seed. It says it grew server capacity 24x for Fortune 500 demand, but names no customers. **Mechanism:** a
   foundation model built for defensive security reads logs continuously and works alerts autonomously.
4. **Everful**: **48.5M dollar Series A** led by Vertex SEA & India and January Capital (2026-11-19). The company says
   it ships **5M+ items a month** to 60+ countries and **expects 100M dollars of annualised revenue by year-end**.
   These figures are company-reported. **Mechanism:** agents find trending products, score factories using its own
   inspection data and run orders, and Everful ships the goods itself.
5. **SPARETECH**: **20M dollar Series B** co-led by Blume Equity and Insight (2026-11-10). **Revenue is up 5x since
   2024** and its catalogue holds 40M+ verified parts. **Mechanism:** AI cleans a manufacturer's spare-parts master data
   against a verified catalogue, so plants share stock and carry less inventory.
6. **Hatz AI**: **24M dollar Series A** (2026-11-12). **Its MSP partner count tripled in a year** and revenue grew
   faster (no amount disclosed); headcount went from 14 to about 60. **Mechanism:** small businesses buy AI agents
   through the IT provider they already trust.

## Meta-signal

This is the sixth straight empty run. Occupancy killed six of seven candidates, and a third of this week's patterns
mapped straight onto earlier kills. **The fresh raises this week cluster in infrastructure (Applied Compute, Corma) and
hardware ecosystems (Skydio, Ring).** In both, the leader's neighbours are large incumbents, not unserved buyers.
Applying the seat-holder shape to physical security turned up a genuine innovator's dilemma, but the incumbents are
already crossing it. **The held insurer and administrator book is still the only live thread, and searches cannot
settle any of it now.** Every remaining question is one a carrier, fund trustee or broker has to answer.
