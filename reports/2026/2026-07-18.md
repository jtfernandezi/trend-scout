## Takeaway for next run

**Five consecutive droughts converts a hypothesis into a settled result: a sixth public-web generative scan will produce a sixth drought, and running it is now negative-expected-value.** The mechanism has been demonstrated five times and again this run — the funding market runs the identical scan with more people and more capital and closes every nameable square within days (this run: Fireworks $1.5B, Emergent $130M, Spectro $100M+, Oak $60M, all inside my two-week window). No refinement of the *query* changes this, because the constraint is the *input source*.

The recommendation is unchanged from the last two runs and now overdetermined — **stop scheduling generative scans; change the input.** Two moves, in priority order:

1. **Run a kill-or-commit pass on the held survivors (strong default).** The ledger holds **9 gated survivors** — ts-0031 (robot-policy assurance lab), ts-0032 (DoD autonomy assurance harness), ts-0039 (pharma-AI credibility lab), **ts-0048 (private-markets liquidity engine, 4.3)**, **ts-0054 (AI-peril cat-model, 4.x)**, ts-0064 (demand-realism), ts-0069 (completion/time-to-power realism), ts-0077 (prediction-market integrity), ts-0086 (compute-supply realism). Note the pattern flagged two runs ago: **six of these are the same "independent correlated-realism" thesis in different buildout chokepoints** (0048/0054/0064/0069/0086 + 0032). The highest-value next slot is not a 10th survivor — it is a dedicated run that (a) adversarially re-validates the top 2-3 against *today's* competitive field, and (b) forces the decision of whether to back **one consolidated systemic-realism company** or the single best instance. That is a *kill-or-commit* run, not a *generate* run.
2. **If the next run must be generative, seed it from a non-public signal.** The only edge a public scan structurally cannot have is private information: a specific institution's unmet internal pain, a founder's domain-insider workflow knowledge, a private RFP, a practitioner-only forum. By construction, any idea visible to this scan is visible to everyone's simultaneously — which is exactly why five scans have produced five droughts.

Zero survivors is the correct, defensible outcome — for the fifth run running — and the pattern is the product: it tells the operator, unambiguously, to stop scanning and either commit to a held idea or feed in a non-public seed. The ledger and digest are updated; no brief this run.
