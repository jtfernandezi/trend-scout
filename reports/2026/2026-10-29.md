down-market or geography flank this run was a config change or already occupied. The one that held was a buyer whose
*entity structure* differs: a union benefit fund has hundreds of employers paying into one plan, while Avante's
schema is one employer. When a winner's schema assumes "one customer equals one organisation," look for buyers that
are federations or pools of organisations.

## Surviving ideas

### ts-0559 — The AI-native fund office: an agent-run administrator for union multiemployer benefit funds

**In plain English.** Millions of union carpenters, electricians and laborers get their health insurance and pensions
from a shared fund. Hundreds of small contractors pay into each fund every month, based on the hours each worker
put in. Someone has to read all those payment reports, check them against the union contract, work out who is
covered this month and chase the contractors who underpay. Today that's done by a few old-fashioned outsourcing firms
with lots of clerks, and workers often wait two months to find out whether they're covered. We'd run that back office
with software that reads and checks everything as it arrives. The fund pays us the same kind of per-member fee it pays
today, and it gets faster coverage, fewer errors and more of the money it's owed.

- **The proven pattern:** Avante raised a 30M dollar Series A (Frist Cressey, late October 2026). It has 70+ employer
  customers and 385,000+ covered lives, bookings are up about 5x, and its agents do employer benefits-administration
  work.
- **Who buys the narrowed version:** the trustees and fund managers of small and mid-sized building-trades health and
  welfare funds, when the administrator contract goes to RFP, the fund office loses staff, or an eligibility backlog
  produces member complaints.
- **Why the leader can't follow:** Avante's data model and business are one employer, one HR feed, sold as SaaS. A
  multiemployer fund has hundreds of employers, hours banks, reciprocity and payroll audits, and it hires a bonded
  service provider through a trustee RFP. The incumbent administrators are PE roll-ups paid per participant for
  manual work, so they face an innovator's dilemma.
- **Expansion path:** building-trades health funds, then the same fund office's pension, annuity and apprenticeship
  trusts, then Teamsters, hospitality and healthcare-worker funds, then public-sector union trusts. About 20-26
  million people are covered, the fee pool is several billion dollars, and claims, pharmacy and audit recoveries
  sit on top.
- **Scores:** Wedge 4 · Pattern 3 · Market 4 · Defensibility 4 · YC-fit 4 · Founder-fit 3 → composite 3.7.
- **Biggest risk:** trustees change administrators slowly and rely on their consultants (Segal and others), so the
  sales cycle runs through RFPs and relationships. Meanwhile, an incumbent TPA could buy off-the-shelf agents and
  keep the savings.

## Method note for the next run

**One. Look for federated buyers.** This run's only survivor came from a buyer that is a pool of many organisations
(a multiemployer trust). Other pooled buyers fit the same shape: cooperatives, group purchasing organisations,
captive insurers, risk pools for public entities, homeowner-association management, joint powers authorities. A
winner whose schema is "one customer equals one organisation" may not reach them. Check the ledger first (ts-0314,
ts-0423 cover parts of the public-entity space).

**Two. Re-check ts-0559 occupancy each run.** The occupancy search found horizontal agent templates (Cassidy) but no
AI-native administrator. Search "Taft-Hartley" with "AI" on IFEBP's November conference exhibitor list.

**Three. Skip for now:** everything on the 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-27 lists, plus Casap, Hyphenate, Vera Health,
OpenEvidence, General Compute, Atlas Bio, Infisical, Re:Build and Machenta. Also skip Pix and instant-payment
disputes, small-business AI accounting, payer medical-policy criteria and veterinary evidence search. Ledgebrook (AI
specialty underwriting, 200M dollars) is fair game for an insurance pass if it reappears with new evidence.
