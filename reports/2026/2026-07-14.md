"Regulation launched → build the compliance tool" is the exact one-step extension Step 2 warns against.

The seam is not obviously crackable by a horizontal-agent newcomer.

## Takeaway for next run

Three consecutive droughts is now a structural finding, not a run of bad luck: **the web-scannable idea surface is exhausted for this method.** Across three runs I have tried (1) crossing this-fortnight's capability with this-fortnight's shift, (2) going "earlier than nameable," and now (3) preferring slow-clock institutional buyers — and all three lose to the same dynamic: *anything a web search can surface as a nameable market already has a funded vendor cohort, because the funding market runs the identical scan with more people and more capital.* The slow-clock refinement specifically failed because slow *buyers* do not produce slow *builders* — compliance vendors pre-position years ahead of a dated deadline.

Two honest strategic options for the operator, both of which change the *input*, not just the query:

1. **Stop generating; start committing.** The ledger already holds ~10 gated survivors (ts-0031/0032/0039/0048/0054/0064/0069/0077/0086 + this cluster), several scoring 4.2–4.3. The marginal value of a 15th survivor found by web scan is now plausibly *lower* than the value of pressure-testing and building one of the existing ones. Consider a "deep-dive / kill-or-commit" run that adversarially re-validates the top 3 held ideas against *today's* competitive field rather than hunting a new one.
2. **Change the signal source away from the public web.** The one edge a public web scan structurally *cannot* have is non-public information: a specific institution's unmet internal pain, a founder's domain-insider knowledge, a private RFP, a workflow you only see from inside a vertical. The next *generative* run should be seeded by a primary source (an operator interview, a specific institution's stated pain, a niche practitioner forum) rather than by public funding/regulatory signals — because by construction, any idea visible to my scan is visible to everyone's.

Zero survivors is the correct, defensible outcome — for the third run running — and the pattern is now the product: it tells the operator the method has hit its ceiling and the next move is to commit to a held idea or to feed in a non-public signal. The ledger and digest are updated; no brief this run.
