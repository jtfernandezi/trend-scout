# Trend Scout — Report 2026-11-03

**Run type:** scheduled research pass (sixty-first run under the venture-scale-gated thesis).
**Outcome:** **Zero surviving ideas. 10 new kills (ts-0569 to ts-0578).** No brief this run. No held-book entry
changes status.

This run followed the 2026-10-31 method note:

- **The skip list was respected.** Serval, Broccoli, Avoca, Mave, Upwind, Collective, Cohere, Casap, Vera Health,
  Bedrock, Kalshi, Foundry Robotics and Pathos repeated on this week's trackers and were not re-pointed. Casap is cited
  only as supporting evidence for two kills.
- **Note two (trigger changes on horizontal agent winners) was worked hard.** Three of the ten kills aimed Fragment's
  exception-resolution mechanism at a one-off event: a named storm (ts-0571), a disaster declaration (ts-0572) and the
  IEEPA tariff-refund window (ts-0574). None survived. Two failed on viability, because the event is shrinking (FEMA
  is moving to formula grants) or has mostly cleared (about 136.6bn dollars of tariff refunds were already accepted).
  The third failed on occupancy.
- **Note one (federation only counts if the entity structure breaks the schema) killed one idea.** A ship manager
  buying for hundreds of single-ship owners is a real schema break, but the planned-maintenance vendor already owns
  that data and has shipped the agent (ts-0570).
- **Note three (re-check ts-0559 and ts-0568 occupancy) was not done again.** It carries over for a third run, and it
  should be the first task next run.

**The finding worth carrying forward: in a vertical, the vendor that holds the system of record blocks the opening,
not the breakout.** Every exception-resolution flank this run was killed by the vertical's own system of record. That
was SpecTec's AMOS for ship spares, KYRO's field-operations platform for storm crews, Enverus's JIB data and
OpenInvoice for oil and gas, and WasteLinq and Wastebits for hazardous-waste profiles. Fragment could not follow into
any of these segments. It did not need to, because the vendor already holding the segment's records shipped the agent
first. **New rule: before arguing that the breakout can't follow, name the segment's system-of-record vendor and check
its last two quarters of releases for an agent.** If it has shipped one, the flank is closed.

1. **Fragment**: **56M dollars** (Series A led by 8VC plus a seed co-led by General Catalyst and A*), announced
   **2026-10-28**. Selling to **dozens of Fortune 1000** manufacturers, distributors and energy companies (named:
   Graybar, CNX). Company-reported: 90%+ of exceptions resolved without a human, about 50% fewer exceptions within
   weeks. It is priced by work done, not per seat. **Category cluster:** Lio (30M dollar Series A, a16z), Didero (30M

   policies from its existing systems, then investigate and resolve each exception end to end.
2. **TrashLab**: **12M dollar growth round led by Ventana, 2026-10-29**. Serves **hundreds of haulers in 40+ states
   and Canada**, and nearly 80% use its Haul-E voice agent (company-reported). **Category cluster:** Hauler Hero (16M
   dollar Series A, February 2026), CurbWaste (28M dollar Series B), CommanderAI (5M dollar seed). **Mechanism:** a
   vertical operating system plus agents that answer calls, take orders, dispatch and bill for independent
   route-based waste haulers.
3. **Tutor Intelligence**: **75M dollar Series B led by a16z, 2026-11-03** (about 117M dollars raised in total).
   Robots operate in **almost four times as many locations** as a year ago, and about a third of renters re-rented.
   It targets 1,000+ robots under contract by end-2027. **Category cluster:** Bedrock, Foundry Robotics, Ultra.
   **Mechanism:** learned manipulation policies plus remote human fallback, rented to manufacturers and logistics
   sites by the hour or per pick.
4. **CarbonAI**: **12M dollars led by Cowboy Ventures** (mid-October, just outside the two-week window). Customers
   include debt funds, mortgage REITs and agency lenders (Greystone named). **Mechanism:** agents underwrite, diligence
   and monitor commercial real estate loans, tracing every output to its source document.
5. **Scarlet**: **50M dollar Series B led by Headline** (late October). It builds automated regulatory and compliance
   infrastructure for AI and software medical devices. **Not re-pointed:** the notified-body and certified-clinical-AI
   flanks were closed at ts-0402 and ts-0518 to ts-0525.
6. **Tandem Health**: **Series B of about 100M dollars** (Scaleup Europe Fund and EQT), with its AI scribe reportedly

## Method note for the next run

**One. Do the held-book re-checks first, before scanning.** For ts-0559, search "Taft-Hartley" plus "AI" and the
IFEBP exhibitor list. For ts-0568, search ACG and PE operating-partner conference sponsors for "AI integration", and
check whether Serval, ShareGate or Quest announce PE or M&A programmes.

**Two. Name the system-of-record vendor before arguing a vertical flank.** For any narrowed segment, find who holds
the segment's records (planned-maintenance system, field-ops platform, industry data pool, permitting system) and read
its recent releases. If it has an agent, kill the idea and move on without a long occupancy search.

**Three. Look for segments where no system of record exists yet.** The run's kills point the other way: the openings
should be where records are still on paper, email and spreadsheets, so the first agent becomes the system of record.
That is what ts-0559 (union fund hours banks) and ts-0568 (two companies' systems in a merger) have in common.

**Four. Skip for now:** everything on the 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-31 lists, plus Fragment, Lio, Didero, Freehand,
TrashLab, Hauler Hero, CurbWaste, Tutor Intelligence, CarbonAI, Scarlet, Tandem, Confido and ClarityCare. Also skip
storm-cost recovery, FEMA Public Assistance, tariff refunds, non-op JIB audit, CAM audits, hazardous-waste brokerage,
hauler roll-ups, vessel procurement and munitions robotics. YC Fall 2026 Demo Day is 2026-12-02, so the batch becomes
the main source from the first December run.
