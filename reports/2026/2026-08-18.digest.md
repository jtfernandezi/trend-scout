**2 survivors, 17 kills.** This run left the AI build-out and scanned five untouched shocks: the collapse of long-term care insurance, Europe deleting a tenth of its chemical industry, the $1T SaaS repricing, the majority-machine web, and the broken federal research grant machine.

**1. Care-horizon underwriting — composite 4.2**
Almost everyone wants to grow old at home, and for most families the house is the money — but the insurance that paid for help has vanished, and the reverse mortgage is legally forbidden from looking at your health, so someone with three years left gets the same offer as someone with twenty. This reads a person's real medical history to work out how long they'll actually stay home, then hands the family cash against the house now, repaid on sale, so they can hire a carer this month.

**2. Capacity now — composite 4.0**
Europe is permanently shutting chemical plants and scrapping the machinery, because working out whether a second-hand reactor could do a different job takes an engineer days per item. Meanwhile US plants are told to wait 2–4 years for the same equipment. This reads the engineering paperwork on thousands of stranded machines, matches them to what new plants need, buys the ones that fit, and sells something money can't otherwise buy: a start date next year.
