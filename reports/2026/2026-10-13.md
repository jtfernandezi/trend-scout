# Trend Scout — Report 2026-10-13

**Run type:** scheduled research pass (fifty-second run under the venture-scale-gated thesis).
**Outcome:** **Zero surviving ideas. 8 new kills (ts-0504 to ts-0511).** No brief this run. No held-book entry
has new evidence.

This run followed the 2026-10-10 method note:

- **Regional sourcing: direct fetching works for two of the four sources.** WebFetch on dealstreetasia.com and
  inc42.com/buzz returned this week's rounds, which general search never did. Techloy's Latin America tag page
  returned 404 and LAVCA returned 403. Neither page had an AI-native breakout with traction this week:
  - DealStreetAsia: MakeO/Zenyum, 10M dollars (oral care); NEU Battery Materials, 7.6M dollars (battery
    recycling); Mesa School of Business, 11.6M dollars.
  - Inc42: Clueso, 10M dollars (AI enterprise video, no traction disclosed); ThinkMetal; NuSynth (speciality
    chemicals).

  **Recommendation:** keep the two working direct fetches as a two-minute first step and drop Techloy and LAVCA.
- **The skip list was respected.** Armadin (255M dollar Series B) and Onyx (113M dollar Series B) were not
  re-pointed, because agent security and offensive agents are on the skip list. Arena was scanned on 2026-10-10.
- **Method note one (name the local system of record first) did not come into play.** No geography flank was
  attempted this run, because every candidate was a US adjacent-buyer flank.

**The finding worth carrying forward: in "AI-native system of record" patterns, every adjacent vertical already
has its own funded AI-native core.** This week's clearest category cluster is in mortgage software. Valon (servicing)
and Vesta (origination) both raised, with hard numbers, to replace mainframe-era mortgage cores with an AI-native
ledger whose agents do the operations work. I re-pointed that mechanism at eight neighbouring buyers. Each one
already had its own occupant or a modern incumbent shipping the same thing:

| Vertical | Occupant or incumbent |
|---|---|
| Credit-union lending | Fuse |
| Auto finance | Salient |
| Private credit | Hypercore |
| Health plans | HealthEdge, which shipped on 2026-10-01 |
| Mortgage QC | ACES |
| Closed life blocks | Zinnia |

The "AI-native core replacement" wave is being funded one vertical at a time, all at once. So re-pointing a winner
at the next vertical along usually means arriving second. The other way in, a service seat, mostly fails because
these incumbents charge per loan, per policy or in basis points rather than billing staff hours. AI therefore
raises their margin, so they have no innovator's dilemma.

## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Valon**: **150M dollar Series D at 2.3bn dollars, 2026-10-05** (Ribbit Capital, a16z), double its previous
   valuation. **More than 200M dollars of contracted ARR within six months** of opening ValonOS to the industry.
   It is under contract to service roughly **one in six US mortgages**. Newrez, Carrington (about 2M liens) and
   ServiceMac are live or migrating. **Mechanism:** an AI-native system of record replaces a servicer's mainframe
   core, and agents on top of it do the operations work (borrower email, payment allocation, escrow analysis).
2. **Vesta**: **30M dollar Series B, 2026-10-08** (Conversion Capital). Customers Pennymac, New American Funding
   and NBKC invested. **Revenue is up 12x year on year**, and lenders on the platform originate **more than 100bn
   dollars a year**. Pennymac cut its origination cost 25%, and agents handle about 40% of platform tasks. The CEO
   credits Claude Sonnet 4.5 with making multi-stage agent workflows practical. **Mechanism:** an AI-native loan
   origination system whose agents underwrite, clear conditions and review closings under lender-set rules.
3. **Stuut**: **52.5M dollar Series B** (Insight Partners, with a16z and M12), about ten months after its Series
   A, 93M dollars raised in total. **More than 150 companies**, including Fortune 50 names, use it. It reports a
   47% cut in days sales outstanding (self-reported). **Mechanism:** agents chase invoices by email, SMS and
   voice, match payments to invoices and post them to the ERP. **Not re-pointed:** supplier-side disputes
   (ts-0412) and collections voice (ts-0293) are already closed, and mid-market AR is crowded.
4. **Nous Research**: **90M dollar Series B at 1.5bn dollars** (2026-10-07). About **36M dollars of annualised
   revenue in mid-September**, expected to pass 100M dollars by year-end (WSJ). **Mechanism:** an open-weight
   agent that enterprises run themselves. **Not re-pointed:** it is a horizontal lab with no buyer to narrow, and
   sovereign or self-hosted agents are crowded.
5. **Siena**: **17M dollar Series A** (York IE). It automates up to 80% of customer interactions for FIGS, SPANX
   and HexClad. **Mechanism:** support and shopping agents for consumer brands. **Not re-pointed:** customer
   service agents are heavily mined in the ledger.

Also scanned and not re-pointed:
- **Hone**: 60M dollar seed at 285M dollars (Benchmark, Index). No traction beyond the valuation.
- **Catalyst**: 30M dollar seed (Sequoia). Pilot trading volume only.
- **Blueprints AI**: bootstrapped, with no revenue disclosed. Permitting is mined (ts-0061).
- **SecondSight**: Series A of undisclosed size.
- **Causa Prima**: startups.gallery lists a 20M dollar seed on 2026-10-13 that I could not verify. Its June

## Method note for the next run

**One. For an "AI-native system of record" breakout, run the occupancy search first, as "AI-native [vertical]
core" or "AI-native [vertical] LOS/servicing/admin startup".** This run, one search per vertical found the
occupant (Fuse, Salient, Hypercore, HealthEdge). Do it before spending time on the wedge argument.

**Two. Add to the seat screen: how do the incumbents bill?** If they bill per unit (per loan, per policy, basis
points or commission), AI raises their margin and there is no innovator's dilemma, so kill the service seat. Only
staff-hour billing (as in ts-0470's SETA firms) creates the block.

**Three. Regional sourcing: keep WebFetch on dealstreetasia.com and inc42.com/buzz and drop Techloy and LAVCA.**
Both working pages give same-day rounds. Neither had an AI-native breakout with traction this week.

**Four. Skip for now:** everything from the 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-10 lists, plus AI-native lending and servicing
cores (residential, commercial, auto, credit union, private credit), health-plan core admin, closed-block life
administration and mortgage AI governance.
