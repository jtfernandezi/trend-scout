# Trend Scout — Report 2026-09-26

**Run type:** scheduled research pass (forty-fifth run under the venture-scale-gated thesis).
**Outcome:** **Zero surviving ideas. 11 new kills (ts-0447 to ts-0457).** No brief this run.

This is the second zero in a row, and it is honest. The week's breakouts were sourced almost entirely
from **ground the ledger had never touched**: Numeral, Heidi, Rightway, Confido, Micro1 and Snorkel
appear nowhere in the 446 prior entries. The words "sales tax", "nexus", "CPG" and "excise" also had
zero prior hits. So the kills below are not re-kills of exhausted ground. They are a first map of a new
territory, and most of it is already occupied.

The run followed the 2026-09-24 method note:

- **The property screen for delegated seats was applied.** Two property-shaped seats were opened: the
  Streamlined Sales Tax Certified Service Provider seat (ts-0450), which is state-compensated and
  recurring, and the FAA Parts Manufacturer Approval (ts-0457), which is transferable and earns money
  repeatedly. **Both really are property, and both are already held.** Five CSPs hold the first, and
  Numeral is applying. HEICO holds 19,500 PMAs, and the AI-native gate fails because testing and airline
  acceptance, not paperwork, set the cost. FIFRA registrations, 510(k)s-as-assets and FCC modular grants
  were screened on the AI-native gate first, as instructed, and all three failed without a search. Each
  depends on physical testing or bench certification. They were not logged.
- **The counterparty search now comes first.** It killed ts-0452 (AI-native SALT firm) and ts-0454
  (cleared expert data) before the four parts were written.
- **The compliance-framework certifier seat was not reopened.**

**The finding worth carrying forward: indirect tax is a category where every structural flank has a
local incumbent that got there first.** Numeral's wedge really is blocked on five axes: the purchase
side (AP rather than AR), product-attribute excise, escheat, Brazil, and the licensed-firm version that
would conflict with its new accounting-partner channel. On every axis the block holds against *Numeral*.
But on every axis a pre-AI company already sits there, and in several cases it is AI-branded: Ryan and
the regional CPA firms, Avalara/IGEN/Avior, Eisen and Sovos, Tributei and ROIT, and Kintsugi. Tax is
fifty rule sets and each one grew its own specialist decades ago. **A structural block against the
leader is worthless in a market where the flanks were settled before the leader existed.** Treat indirect
tax as mined.

## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Numeral**: **100M dollar Series C led by Insight Partners, 2026-09-23** (157.5M dollars raised
   since its 2023 founding; Benchmark, Mayfield, YC, Salesforce Ventures). Traction: **transaction
   volume up 327 percent year on year, 3,500+ businesses, 80M+ transactions expected**, VAT/GST in 90+
   countries, and a new accounting-partner programme. **Mechanism:** agents with humans in the loop
   turn multi-jurisdiction indirect-tax registration, calculation and filing into a subscription. This
   was a professional-services job until models could read changing rules and messy transaction data.

2. **Heidi**: **100M dollar Series C at 900M dollars (Blackbird) plus a 240M dollar General Catalyst
   growth investment, 2026-09-22.** Traction: **ARR grew from 1M to 50M dollars in two years**, 175M+
   patient visits supported (up from 73M at Series B), 190 countries, 110 languages. **Mechanism:**
   ambient capture turns a clinical conversation into the record, and now into supervised actions
   (orders, referrals, pre-visit synthesis) that the clinician signs. Notably, the agentic features
   **exclude the UK and EU at launch** for regulatory reasons.

3. **Rightway**: **155M dollar Series E led by Francisco Partners, 2026-09-24** (1.75bn dollar last
   valuation; IPO talk). Traction: **45 Fortune 500 clients.** **Mechanism:** a pharmacy benefit
   manager (PBM) that is paid a fixed fee, passes rebates through and owns no pharmacies, paired with
   clinical navigation. The conflicts in the PBM business model become the selling point.

4. **Confido**: **55M dollar Series B led by Insight Partners, 2026-09-22.** Traction: **5x growth
   since its Series A, 250+ consumer brands** (Unilever, Dude Wipes, Kettle & Fire); expanding into
   foodservice. **Mechanism:** agents reconcile the consumer-packaged-goods commercial cycle (trade
   promotion, retailer deductions, broker commissions) across finance and sales.

5. **The expert-data cluster.** **Micro1: 100M+ dollars at a 4bn dollar valuation** (reported
   2026-09-22), **500M dollars annualized revenue in August, up from 125M dollars at end-2025**; frontier
   labs, Microsoft, Amazon and 1X are customers. **Snorkel AI: 350M dollar Series E at 3.2bn dollars,
   375M+ dollars ARR** (2026-09-23). **Mechanism:** labs pay per hour for vetted expert judgement and
   environments, because post-training is now bottlenecked on expert signal rather than raw data.

6. **The AI drug-discovery cluster.** **Enveda 311M dollar Series E, Basecamp Research 140M dollar
   Series C, BigHat 75M dollar Series C**: 526M dollars in one week. **Mechanism:** model-guided design

## Method note for the next run

**One. Check a category's age before working its flanks.** Numeral's category (indirect tax) is older
than software; every jurisdiction and every tax type grew a specialist firm decades ago. In such
categories, a flank that is blocked against the AI leader is almost always held by a pre-AI local. The
cheap filter: before developing a flank of an *old* category, search "[flank] software" and
"[flank] services firm" together. If both return established names, drop it without writing four parts.
Flanks are most likely to be open in categories whose job did not exist before 2023 (agents, AI
infrastructure, new regulatory regimes).

**Two. Chase supply-side and new-job openings, not buyer-side ones.** Two runs in a row, the
buyer-side flanks of a hot category (small-employer health, then indirect tax) were occupied. The one
recent seam that held (ts-0424) was a *seat that produces something*. Next run: take this week's
breakouts that create a new job (Heidi's supervised clinical actions, SpaceFlow's procurement agents,
OpenRelay's routing across chips) and ask who must now **sign for, insure or supply** what the agent
does, not who else might buy the agent.

**Three. Skip for now:** indirect tax (all flanks), expert-data clearance (Scale), veterinary AI-bio
(config), and medical interpreting (legally capped until a statute defines a machine interpreter).
