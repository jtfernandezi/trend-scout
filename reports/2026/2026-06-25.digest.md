*1. The correlated-AI-failure catastrophe model for primary insurers ("the RMS / CyberCube for the silent-AI peril").*
Almost every company now uses AI, so when AI causes harm, insurers get claims — and they're terrified that because thousands of companies rely on the same few AI models, all could fail at once and bury them in simultaneous claims. So they're just refusing to cover AI at all. This builds the tool that lets an insurer actually measure that "everyone fails together" risk across its customers, so it can price and sell AI coverage instead of slamming the door.
