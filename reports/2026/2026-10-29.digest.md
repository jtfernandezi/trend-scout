*Trend Scout — 2026-10-29*

*1 survivor, 5 kills.* This is the first new idea since 2026-10-01.

*The survivor.*
- *The AI-native fund office: an agent-run administrator for union multiemployer benefit funds.* Hundreds of small
  contractors pay into each union health and pension fund every month. We'd run the back office that reads those
  payments, checks them against the union contract and decides who is covered, with software instead of rooms of
  clerks, so workers get coverage faster and funds collect what they're owed.
  _For:_ trustees of union carpenter, electrician and laborer funds, when their administrator contract comes up for
  bid. Composite 3.7.

*What we tried.* This week's winners were Casap (AI for card disputes, 56M dollars), Hyphenate (AI finance team,
revenue up about 80x), Avante (AI for company benefits teams, 70+ customers) and Vera Health (AI medical answers,
800,000 clinicians).

*What died, in plain English.*
- Pix fraud refunds for Brazilian banks: local providers already sell it.
- Card-dispute AI for credit unions: Casap already sells to them.
- AI accounting for small businesses: very crowded.
- AI-written insurance approval rules: the two incumbents just add AI.
- AI medical answers for vets: several startups already do it.

*Lesson.* Openings show up where a winner's software assumes one customer is one company, and the buyer is a pool
of many.
