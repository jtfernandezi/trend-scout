## Surviving idea

### ts-0048 — The redemption-run prediction & liquidity-orchestration engine for semi-liquid private funds ("the stress-test brain that keeps a private fund from gating")

**In plain English.** Wall Street has spent five years selling ordinary investors funds that own hard-to-sell things — private loans, private companies, real estate — while promising you can pull your money out whenever you want. The problem: when too many people ask for their money back at the same time (which is happening *right now* — Apollo just slammed the window shut today, Blackstone last week), the fund can't sell its slow assets fast enough, so it locks everyone in. This company builds the system that predicts those stampedes before they happen — it learns how real investors actually behave when they get scared, models the whole crowd pulling at once, and tells the fund exactly how much easy-to-sell cash to keep on hand so it never has to lock the doors. Funds pay for it because getting caught flat-footed (like Apollo did) is a reputational and regulatory disaster — and the *real* prize is the wave of retirement money about to flood in: the government is opening the $12-trillion 401(k) system to these exact funds, and they legally cannot let it in until they can prove they won't trap a retiree's savings.

- **Who buys it.** *Beachhead:* the liquidity/portfolio-risk lead at an asset manager running a retail-sold semi-liquid fund (non-traded BDC, interval fund, evergreen) — acutely, the next 30 firms watching Apollo gate and realizing their own redemption models are wrong. *Expansion:* the asset managers, recordkeepers and target-date/CIT providers building the first private-asset 401(k) sleeves, who must prove to ERISA fiduciaries that participant withdrawals can be honored before the DOL rule lets them in.
- **Why no one already serves them.** Institutional risk platforms (BlackRock Aladdin, MSCI LiquidityMetrics) model whether the *holdings* can be sold under assumed redemption rates — not the *correlated, reflexive, panic-driven run behavior* that actually breaks these funds. Valuation players (73 Strings, Canoe) mark assets, not flows. Corporate-treasury AI cash-forecasting is the wrong buyer. The managers themselves were caught flat-footed — their models assumed redemptions would stay "limited and predictable." Nobody owns the behavioral run-prediction + liquidity-orchestration layer.
- **Scores:** Novelty 4 · Why-now 5 · Market 5 · Defensibility 4 · YC-fit 4 · Founder-fit 4 → **composite 4.3**.
- **Biggest risk.** **AI-native gate + data access.** Two-sided: (1) a skeptic can call this "sophisticated quant/ML, not frontier AI" — the answer is that correlated, news-reactive, herding run-prediction across millions of heterogeneous investors is exactly what deep sequence models unlock and what classical independent-rate actuarial models structurally get wrong (which is *why* Apollo/Blue Owl got caught); and (2) the behavioral moat needs investor/participant flow data the managers and recordkeepers control — so the wedge is winning a design-partner manager *now*, in the post-Apollo panic, and compounding a cross-fund flow dataset before Aladdin/MSCI or a fund-services incumbent (SS&C) notice the run-prediction layer is a product.

## Takeaway for next run

The 2026-06-20 instruction to **leave the assurance vein and hunt a fresh capital-markets shift** worked: the single survivor came from a structural shift (private markets → the $12T DC system) that had never been crossed with AI in this ledger, and the winning cross was a **half-step the funded crowd skipped** (flows, not marks). 1. **In a hot shift, map who already owns each adjacent square, then take the one they skipped.** Valuation was taken (73 Strings); fiduciary paperwork was taken (Aon/Morningstar); monitoring was taken (credit-tech). Run-prediction was empty — and it's the one the live crisis is actually about.
2. **A live, dated trigger beats a pending one.** Apollo gating *today* turns a "coming-regulation" thesis into a "phones-ringing-now" sale. Next run, keep watching the private-credit/semi-liquid stress (more gates likely) and the DOL final rule; and pressure-test one more untouched big shift — the **GENIUS-Act stablecoin rails** (corporate-treasury adoption curve) or the **AI-roll-up holdco** layer — for a cross the headline-chasers have skipped.
