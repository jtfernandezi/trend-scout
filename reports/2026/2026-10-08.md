# Trend Scout — Report 2026-10-08

**Run type:** scheduled research pass (fiftieth run under the venture-scale-gated thesis).
**Outcome:** **Zero surviving ideas. 6 new kills (ts-0490 to ts-0495).** No brief this run. One evidence update on
ts-0336 (status unchanged).

This run followed the 2026-10-06 method note:

- **I tried to start from Southeast Asia, Latin America and biomanufacturing, as the note asked. It found nothing
  usable.** The search index had no October rounds for either region. The latest Latin American AI Series A it
  found is Telepatia (33M dollars, a16z, July). Southeast Asia's 2026 AI funding is 4.1bn dollars, but Kling's
  2.8bn dollar round is about 68% of it, and deal count is falling. The biomanufacturing names (ChemT, 4M dollar
  seed in June; Multiply Labs, below) were too early or already in the ledger. **The thin regions are thin in the
  data too, so this has to be done with region-specific sources (DealStreetAsia, LAVCA, Techloy) when they publish
  Q3, not with general search.**
- **The breakouts therefore came from this week's US and European tape.** A grep found none of Healthleap, Santé,
  Verso, Miravoice, Avarra, Mecka, Rein, Monid or Sunfox among the 489 prior entries.
- **The three-question seat screen killed ts-0494 before the occupancy search mattered.** A public survey seat
  passes "incumbents bill labour". It fails "is it open now", because the buyer needs a methodology authority's
  approval before it can buy a new interview mode.

**The finding worth carrying forward: when a regulator must approve the *method* before the buyer can buy it, the
seat is closed, however labour-heavy the incumbents are.** The ledger's surviving seats (ts-0470 SETA, ts-0424
ODA, ts-0336 patient interviewing) all have a buyer who can contract this year under rules that already accept
the output. BRFSS states cannot switch to a machine interviewer without CDC, and federal surveys cannot without OMB
statistical standards and mode-effect studies. So add a fourth question to the seat screen: **(d) does the buyer
need someone else's methodological approval before it can buy?** If yes, kill it.

## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Healthleap**: **38M dollars seed plus Series A, 2026-10-07** (Sequoia and First Round seed; Hummingbird led
   the 30M dollar Series A). **3 to 50+ hospitals in a year, revenue up more than 10x.** Customers include Penn
   Medicine, Cedars-Sinai, Houston Methodist, Emory and Intermountain, with 23.8M dollars of annualised impact
   reported at HUP. **Mechanism:** a model reads every inpatient's chart daily and finds conditions nobody has
   diagnosed yet, starting with malnutrition. The hospital is paid through reimbursement and shorter stays.
2. **Mecka**: **60M dollar Series B, 2026-10-07** (Sequoia; Nvidia, M12, Qualcomm, Samsung). **More than 100M
   dollars of annualised revenue in June, projecting 300M dollars by year-end.** **Mechanism:** captured human
   motion sold as training data for robot models. Not re-pointed, because egocentric and teleoperation data for
   robotics has five prior kills in the ledger (ts-0036, 0103, 0125, 0174, 0205).
3. **Santé**: **15M dollar Series A, 2026-10-07** (FINTOP; YC), six months after a 7.6M dollar seed. **Nearly
   1,000 independent wine and spirits retailers, about 2bn dollars of GMV, 500% growth.** **Mechanism:** one
   till, web shop and inventory system with agents that run an independent retailer's marketing, in a regulated,
   deep-catalogue category that horizontal tills serve poorly.
4. **Rein Security**: **25M dollar Series A, 2026-10-08** (Glilot, Sienna). **Revenue up 8x and customers up 5x
   since its January launch** (Dun & Bradstreet, Lemonade). **Mechanism:** security for AI agents while they run.
   Not re-pointed, because agent security is mined (ts-0054, ts-0178).
5. **Verso and Miravoice**, an AI-interviewing cluster. Verso: **6M dollar seed, 2026-10-08**, 30+ organisations
   (Ferrero, Orange, Michelin). Miravoice: **6.3M dollar seed** (Unusual Ventures), 10-20 customers running phone
   surveys of 120+ questions, with research published alongside SSRS and NORC. **Mechanism:** a model conducts
   and codes long interviews by voice, so human interviewers stop limiting sample size.
6. **Avarra**: **17M dollar Series A, 2026-10-06** (Duration; Lightspeed, Zoom). **ARR up 400%+, contracted
   enterprise ARR up 698%, 10,000+ sessions a week.** **Mechanism:** a model plays the customer in sales roleplay
   and scores the rep.
7. **Nous Research** (90M dollar Series B at 1.5bn dollars; revenue from 36M dollars annualised in mid-September

**Patterns worked and whether they are exhausted:**
- **Healthleap (find what the chart missed): both flanks are held.** The SNF version is a known MDS/PDPM category,
  and the denial-defence version belongs to Iodine. The idea worth testing later is outpatient and home care,
  which Healthleap names as its own roadmap. That makes it a config-change kill in advance, so skip it.
- **Santé (vertical OS for independent regulated retail): the wedge is real, the segments are small.** Every
  category that horizontal tills exclude (firearms, cannabis, pharmacy, alcohol) already has a vertical incumbent
  or caps out well below 1bn dollars. ts-0299 (independent pharmacy) died the same way.
- **AI interviewing (Verso, Miravoice, Conveo, Simile): the commercial side is crowded and the public side is
  gated.** ts-0336's regulated clinical seat is still the only version with an open buyer.
- **AI roleplay (Avarra): no structural wedge in any regulated flank found.** Training budgets buy horizontal tools.

## Method note for the next run

**One. Add question (d) to the seat screen: does the buyer need someone else's approval of the method before it
can buy?** If a standards body, protocol owner or statistical authority must first approve the method (CDC for
BRFSS, OMB for federal surveys, a payer-defined coding rule), the seat is closed regardless of incumbent billing.
Ask it before the occupancy search.

**Two. Regional sourcing needs regional sources.** General search returned nothing from Southeast Asia or Latin
America newer than July. Next run, go straight to DealStreetAsia's Q3 2026 deal review, LAVCA, Techloy's
weekly Latin America roundups and Inc42 (India), and only then to the general tape.

**Three. Skip for now:** chart-reading condition detection (hospital, SNF and denial flanks), vertical tills for
excluded retail categories, AI sales or compliance roleplay, AI-moderated research (except ts-0336's regulated
seat), robotics training data, agent runtime security and agent payments.
