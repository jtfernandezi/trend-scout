*Trend Scout — 2026-11-10*

*0 survivors, 9 kills.* No idea passed all three tests this run, and none was pushed through to fill the slot.

*What we tried.* This week's winners were Sumary (AI that does accounting firms' routine work, 25M dollars, signed by Deloitte and most top Nordic firms), Finite State (AI that finds security holes in device software, 27M dollars), Nominal (software for hardware test data, 240M dollars), Alterra Labs (doctor-designed training exercises for AI, 12.5M dollars), Guava (voice agents for banks and insurers) and Onodrim (buys European defence suppliers).

*What died, in plain English.*
- An AI-run accounting firm for UK and Nordic small businesses: several funded companies already do this.
- Sumary's idea for Japanese tax accountants: Japan's two main accounting-software makers launched the same AI in September.
- Checking imported gadgets against the new EU cyber law: a German company already sells exactly this.
- Insurance for gadget makers under the new EU product-liability law: existing tech insurers can simply add a security scan.
- Deal insurance for small company sales: specialist insurers already removed the fees that kept small deals out.
- Insurance against a key supplier shutting down: the big broker Marsh already maps clients' suppliers for this.
- Nominal's approach for drug factories: several startups already work inside big drug companies.
- Doctor-designed AI training in other languages: the firms that supply AI labs with experts already hire doctors worldwide.
- A European-controlled version of Nominal for defence: Nominal can already install privately, and European test-equipment makers hold the data.

*Also.* Last run's survivor, insurance for mid-size builders against a specialist firm going bust, still holds up: insurers still sell that cover only to builders above 75-150M dollars.

*Lesson.* Accounting-firm AI is now taken in every major market we've checked. Insurance products that are only closed to small buyers because of cost are being opened up by specialist insurers. Next stop: YC's Fall 2026 batch ahead of Demo Day on December 2.
