This run followed the 2026-10-08 method note:

- **I tried regional sources first, as the note asked: DealStreetAsia, Techloy and Inc42.** Search still returns
  nothing from them newer than late September. The latest DealStreetAsia review is H1 2026 (7.25bn dollars across
  217 deals, the lowest deal count since 2018). The newest Inc42 weekly is 21-25 September, and the newest Techloy
  Latin America roundup is from June. The only regional breakout on this week's tape was **Scanntech**, a
  Uruguayan company that turns supermarket till data into market intelligence. It was not re-pointed, because AI
  is not the core of what it does. **Two runs in a row now show that general search cannot reach the regional
  tape.** Next run should fetch those publishers' pages directly or drop the instruction.
- **The skip list was respected.** Arena (AI model evaluation, below) is the week's strongest traction signal,
  but evaluation flanks were closed in ts-0043, ts-0100 and ts-0479, so it was scanned and not re-pointed.
  EliseAI already has five kills (ts-0061, ts-0269, ts-0467 to ts-0469).
- **Question (d) of the seat screen** (does the buyer need someone else's approval of the method?) never came
  into play. No candidate this run was a service seat; all eight were software or product flanks.

**The finding worth carrying forward: when the US leader is blocked by sovereignty, the opening usually belongs
to the local system of record, not to a new startup.** The Gallatin flank (ts-0496) had the strongest structural
block of the run. The Bundeswehr has said Palantir is "not being considered", Swiss and other European bodies have
refused it, and SAFE requires EU design authority for AI and C4ISTAR products. Gallatin runs on Palantir Foundry
and cannot follow. But the European seat is already held by Systematic's SitaWare (NATO's land C2 standard, built
for sovereign deployment, already showing agentic planning), Comand AI (Saab-backed) and SAP (the Bundeswehr's
logistics ERP). Each of them is one module away from Gallatin's job. This is the 2026-10-06 lesson from the other
direction: a geography wedge only works if no local incumbent already owns the workflow.

## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Gallatin AI**: **50M dollar Series A, 2026-10-08** (8VC, Silent Ventures), 70M dollars raised in total,
   18 months after a 15M dollar seed. Navigator is used by Army and Air Force units in exercises in Japan, Alaska
   and Europe, and Gallatin holds an Army PORTAL Direct-to-Phase-II prototype contract. Revenue is not disclosed.
   **Mechanism:** a model fuses unit logistics data to forecast fuel, munitions and medical demand and plan
   resupply in contested conditions, built on Palantir Foundry.
2. **Arena**: **200M dollar Series B at 3.1bn dollars, 2026-10-08** (Lightspeed, Khosla). **Annualised revenue
   above 100M dollars, up from about 30M dollars in January.** **Mechanism:** crowdsourced head-to-head model
   comparisons and real agent sessions (90,000+ across 27 models) turned into evaluations that labs and
   enterprises pay for. Not re-pointed, because evaluation is on the skip list.
3. **Basalt Health**: **20M dollar Series A** (NEA), late September. At Lifepoint, median referral-processing time
   fell **from 8.5 to 1.2 minutes**, and Basalt is rolling out to **111 hospital markets by year-end** (Lifepoint
   and ScionHealth). **Mechanism:** a model reads faxed and portal referral packets and checks them against the
   receiving facility's admission criteria, beds and payer rules.
4. **Penelope Health**: **100M dollar commitment led by Thoreau**, September. It covers policies affecting
   **200M+ Americans and 15,000+ procedure and drug codes.** **Mechanism:** a model turns insurers' written
   coverage policies into structured, machine-queryable rules.
5. **Gravis Robotics**: **200M dollar Series A led by SoftBank**, August 2026, nine months after a 23M dollar
   round. Its kits are installed on six major OEM brands and deployed on four continents, and it leads an 8M
   dollar UK government project. Paid install count is not disclosed. **Mechanism:** a brand-agnostic retrofit
   kit plus learned excavation policies makes mixed earthmoving fleets autonomous.
6. **Osavul**: **8.5M euro Series A, 2026-10-01** (33N Ventures). **Contract value grew fourfold in 2025**, and
   Osavul is a partner in the NATO information-environment consortium. **Mechanism:** a model reads social, news
   and messaging channels, finds coordinated influence operations, and links them to physical threats against
   a client's assets.
7. **Ledgebrook**: **200M dollar primary equity, 2026-10-07** (Allianz X, Rockefeller), plus a multi-year Allianz
   Re treaty. **On track for 1bn dollars of cumulative premium since 2023**, rated A- by AM Best. **Mechanism:**
   an AI underwriting platform reads, classifies and prices mid-size excess-and-surplus liability submissions in
   hours. Not re-pointed, because nine prior MGA and carrier kills (ts-0016 to ts-0309) mark that ground as
   crowded, and I found no segment Ledgebrook's wholesale-only model structurally excludes.

## Method note for the next run

**One. Before working a geography or sovereignty flank, name the local system of record.** If a local incumbent
(Systematic, SAP, a national champion) already owns the workflow the foreign leader is locked out of, the opening
is theirs. Kill the idea before the occupancy search.

**Two. Regional sourcing: fetch the publishers' pages directly or stop asking.** Two runs of general search
returned nothing newer than late September. Try WebFetch on dealstreetasia.com, techloy.com/tag/latin-america
and inc42.com/buzz before the US tape. If that also fails, drop the instruction.

**Three. Skip for now:** everything from the 2026-10-08 list, plus prior authorisation and payer-policy data
(provider, payer, device and oncology flanks are all held), referral and admission intake, narrative and
disinformation intelligence, and military logistics.
