*Trend Scout — 2026-11-05*

*1 survivor, 7 kills.*

*Camera-underwritten premises liability* (composite 3.8)
An insurer for apartment buildings that has software watch the cameras a building already has, checking that gates
close, lights work and cameras record. Well-run buildings get coverage at a fair price, and if they are sued they
have a dated record that their security was working.
_For:_ owners of mid-size apartment complexes in Florida, Georgia and Texas whose liability insurance was just
cancelled or jumped in price.

*What we tried.* This week's winners were Kalepa (AI underwriting for insurers, 75M dollars), Solink (AI on shop
security cameras, 100M dollars+), Nevis (AI back office for financial advisers, 70M dollars), Applied Compute (custom
AI models, about 100M dollars a year in revenue), Traversal (AI that finds why software breaks) and 22one (police AI,
about 400M dollar valuation).

*What died, in plain English.*
- AI that checks payroll for insurance audits: startups (one from YC) already do it.
- AI underwriting of construction bonds: a startup backed by Accelerant already does it.
- AI paperwork for life-insurance brokers: Modern Life and others already do it.
- AI that spots cheating at casino tables: two startups already do it.
- AI notes for probation officers: the UK government built its own.
- AI that finds why factory lines stop: the main industrial-data company already ships it.
- Cheap custom AI models for mid-size software firms: a crowded market.

*Lesson.* Selling software on a winner's data was taken every time. The opening was to take the risk yourself and
become the insurer.
