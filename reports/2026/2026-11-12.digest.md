*Trend Scout — 2026-11-12*

*0 survivors, 8 kills.* No idea passed all three tests this run, and none was pushed through to fill the slot.

*What we tried.* This week's winners were Path Robotics (welding robots, 300M dollars, revenue up 17x), Blue J (AI tax research, 176M dollars, 6,500 firms), Daylight (AI-plus-human cyber defence, 80M dollars, revenue up 40x), Charta Health (checks medical bills before they go to insurers, 55M dollars), Abby Care (pays family members to be trained caregivers, 82M dollars) and Mount Thor (rents Mac computers to AI agents, 45M dollars).

*What died, in plain English.*
- An AI firm that settles people's IRS tax debts: several services already do this, and the whole industry is small.
- Blue J for Indian accountants: India's main tax-law publisher launched the same AI with EY.
- Answering IRS letters about crypto gains: the crypto tax apps already hold the records and offer this.
- Buying doctors' insurance claims upfront and taking the risk of denial: a payments firm backed by Claritev is already doing it.
- Checking hospice records before Medicare audits: a funded startup, Brellium, does it and even pays back penalties it misses.
- AI-run cyber defence for European mid-size firms under the new EU security law: Eye Security and others already sell it, insurance included.
- Paid family caregiving for elderly parents: Careforth and Momba already do it, and Abby Care can expand there.
- Secure virtual computers for AI agents at banks: plenty of providers already run these in customers' own clouds.

*Also.* An older idea, an AI-run medical provider for county jails (ts-0381), moved up to "watching". Oura's 625M dollar purchase of an AI doctor company puts a price on the approach, and counties are still re-bidding jail contracts after the biggest provider went bankrupt.

*Lesson.* In healthcare billing, software companies now guarantee results themselves, so "become the insurer" is no longer an opening there. A rival's channel conflict only helps if nobody else already sells that result to the buyer.
