*1. Attestation — the competence engine for supervised practice (composite 4.2)*
America is short of doctors while tens of thousands of fully trained ones already live here, barred
because they never did an American residency — and 27 states have just changed the law so they can
practise if they work two supervised years and someone credibly certifies they're good enough, which
almost nobody manages because a small rural hospital has no spare senior doctor to watch them and
there's no accepted way to test the skill. We test the doctor, place them with a hospital that
desperately needs one, then read every note, order and prescription they write by machine and escalate
only the cases a human supervisor actually needs to see.
*For:* the chief medical officer of a rural or critical-access hospital in one of the 13 states with a
live pathway, sitting on a vacancy that failed a full recruiting cycle — and the state programmes
themselves, which have applicants queued and no sponsoring employers. Illinois: 24 applicants under
review, 4 licences issued.
