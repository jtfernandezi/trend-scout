# Trend Scout — Report 2026-10-06

**Run type:** scheduled research pass (forty-ninth run under the venture-scale-gated thesis).
**Outcome:** **Zero surviving ideas. 7 new kills (ts-0483 to ts-0489).** No brief this run.

This run followed the 2026-10-03 method note:

- **Sourcing started outside the US funding tape.** Five of the seven breakouts are European: Vocca, Pandektes,
  Procuros, calliora and Parallel, and Finovox. A grep found none of Vocca, Pandektes, Procuros, calliora, Vinci,
  Spiko, WhiteLab or Finovox in the 482 prior entries. The Japanese and Indian searches gave no new breakout with
  disclosed revenue this week. Sakana's Series B is old news, and Ema was already scanned.
- **The two-question screen was applied to a government seat first.** I tested the CMS Medicare Advantage audit
  (RADV) medical-record review seat. It passes both questions: the conflict is enforced, and the incumbents bill
  labor. It still died on size (ts-0485). CMS gave the seat to FedPath Solutions, with work starting 2026-09-30, on
  a task order worth roughly 18-28M dollars. **The two questions are necessary but not sufficient. A third question
  belongs before the search: is the seat worth more than 100M dollars a year, and is it open now?**
- **The "skip" list was respected.** No offensive-security, judiciary, model-evaluation, AML-testing, RMBS,
  secret-shopper or independent-engineer flanks were reopened. Hadrian (40M dollars) and Fleuret AI (EUR 4M) were
  scanned and not re-pointed because offensive agents are exhausted.

**The finding worth carrying forward: when a European breakout copies a US category, the US version of its narrowed
idea is usually already funded.** Procuros' US flank belongs to Orderful (35M dollar Series C in June 2026),
Pandektes' to Trellis, and Vinci's to PhysicsX (300M dollars at 2.4bn dollars in June 2026). The European
companies are themselves the "geography" wedge on a US pattern. Re-pointing them back at the US just finds the
original. The useful move with a European breakout is to ask what its *European* regulatory seat looks like. That
seat is often a public body (Germany's Medical Service, NHS 111), which kills the venture-scale gate instead.

## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Vocca** (Paris and New York): **20M dollar Series A, 2026-10-06** (Norrsken VC, Heal Capital, Speedinvest).
   **2,000 to 15,000 practitioners in a year, revenue up 7x**, and 70% of calls resolved without a human (85% at
   mature customers). **Mechanism:** a voice agent runs a medical practice's phone line end to end, because speech
   models can now hold a whole admin conversation and write into the scheduling system.
2. **Pandektes** (Copenhagen): **EUR 13.5M Series A, 2026-10-05** (Alstin Capital). **Revenue up 6x in 12 months**,
   500+ law firms, companies and public bodies in DK, DE and CH, and two-thirds of trials convert.
   **Mechanism:** AI continuously collects and links fragmented legislation and rulings that publishers never
   structured.
3. **Procuros** (Berlin): **EUR 20M Series A, 2026-10-06** (XAnge, Point Nine, Creandum). **300+ customers, EUR 3B
   annualised order volume.** **Mechanism:** AI translates orders, invoices and shipping data between incompatible
   ERP and EDI systems, replacing per-connection mapping projects.
4. **Parallel and calliora**, a European hospital-coding cluster. Parallel (Paris): **20M dollar Series A** (Index
   Ventures, YC), live in public and private hospitals. calliora (Munich): **6M dollar pre-seed, 2026-10-06**
   (Bertelsmann Healthcare Investments, redalpine), live in a double-digit number of hospitals across five German
   states. **Mechanism:** agents check each inpatient case against coding rules and auditors' patterns before
   billing. **Why now:** Germany's audit quota becomes error-rate-linked in 2027, rising to 100% of bills for the
   worst hospitals.
5. **Vinci** (Palo Alto): **250M dollars at 1.5bn dollars, 2026-10-06** (Advent, Temasek, Xora; Khosla, Eclipse).
   Traction is thin: it is going **from two pilot deployments to twenty**. **Mechanism:** AI physics solvers fast
   enough to sit inside the chip-design loop. Counted as a pattern only because PhysicsX (300M dollars at 2.4bn
   dollars, June 2026) confirms the category.
6. **Finovox** (Paris): **EUR 8.2M Series A after tripling revenue.** **Mechanism:** machine-scale detection of
   forged documents, because generative AI made forgeries trivial.
7. **Spiko** (Paris): **90M dollar Series B, 2026-10-06** (NEA, Index). **2.7bn dollars of assets, up more than 5x
   year on year, 10,000+ clients.** **Mechanism:** regulated money-market funds issued on public blockchains with
   APIs. Not re-pointed because it fails the AI-native gate.

## Method note for the next run

**One. Add a size-and-openness question to the seat screen.** Before any occupancy search on a reviewer or auditor
seat, ask three things. (a) Is the conflict enforced by disqualification? (b) Do incumbents bill labor? (c) Is the
seat worth more than 100M dollars a year, *and* is it open for award in the next 12 months? ts-0485 passed (a) and
(b) and died on (c).

**Two. Treat a European breakout as evidence for a *European* seat, not a US clone.** Before re-pointing a European
company at the US, search the US category leader by name. This run, Orderful, Trellis and PhysicsX each took a
single search to find. Where the European seat is a public body, kill on the venture-scale gate without searching.

**Three. Source from categories this ledger has least worked.** Grep counts show that Japan (3 entries), Indonesia
(0), Brazil and Mexico (2 each) and pharma manufacturing are thin. Next run, start from Q3 2026 Southeast Asia and
Latin America funding reports, and from pharma CDMO and biomanufacturing rounds with disclosed revenue.

**Four. Skip for now:** patient-access voice (all flanks), German hospital-bill review, CMS claims-review contractor
seats, AI EDI mapping, trial-court data, AI physics simulation flanks, and deepfake-evidence forensics.
