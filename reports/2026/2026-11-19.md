## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Arva AI**: a **20M dollar Series A led by Arthur Ventures**, with YC and Gradient (announced 2026-11-17).
   Customers include a top-10 US bank, an S&P 500 payments company and Wise, and a Fiserv partnership reaches banks and
   credit unions. **In 2026, customers began letting the agents make the final decision on alerts.** No revenue was
   disclosed. **Mechanism:** agents research the party behind a sanctions or money-laundering alert and close it with
   an audit trail.
2. **Boltz**: a **110M dollar Series A led by Bessemer** (Endpoints, 2026-11-18). Six of the 20 largest pharma companies
   are customers (Pfizer, GSK, Takeda and BMS are named), and **paid token volume grew 50x since June.**
   **Mechanism:** open biomolecular models plus a paid platform let drug designers screen and design molecules
   computationally.
3. **Humyn Labs** (Bengaluru): **47M dollars** (26M dollars of equity led by Accel and 21M dollars of venture debt,
   2026-11-18). Forbes, as syndicated, reports **more than 50M dollars of annualised revenue within six months.**
   **Mechanism:** pay people to record themselves doing physical tasks, and sell the footage as robot training data.
4. **GC AI**: the company says it has **passed 40M dollars of ARR, up 4x year on year, with 2,200+ company customers**
   (company press release). Sacra estimated about 20M dollars in mid-2026. Its last round was a 60M dollar Series B at
   555M dollars. **Mechanism:** a self-serve legal assistant lets one in-house lawyer do a department's work.
5. **Xlue**: a **35M dollar seed led by Conviction** (2026-11-18), a Carnegie Mellon spinout. **No traction was
   disclosed**, so it is a weak pattern used only for one candidate. **Mechanism:** predict cancer and disease risk
   from longitudinal health records.
6. **BrightAI**: revenue above **100M dollars** and 250,000 AI endpoints at 50,000+ locations (company, 2025). A
   reported new 97M dollar round could not be confirmed. **Mechanism:** sensors plus AI on water, power and other
   essential-service assets turn field operations into continuous monitoring.

Also scanned and not re-pointed:

## Held book

- **ts-0594 (construction subcontractor-default insurer): weakened, status unchanged.** Hudson's SDI product, in
  market since 2018, targets GCs with **more than 50M dollars of revenue**. The 2026-11-10 re-check recorded 75M
  dollars. A broker page (ALKEME, 2026-04) says some GCs with 25-50M dollars of revenue have run programmes. American
  Global launched an SDI administration module (C-RISQ, 2026-10-26); it is admin software, not an underwriter, and
  makes no AI claim. The uncovered beachhead therefore shrinks to GCs under about 50M dollars of revenue, and the top
  of the original beachhead overlaps an incumbent's appetite. **The settling fact is whether a GC under 50M dollars of
  revenue can carry any retention.** If not, the idea becomes a per-sub bond replacement, which is a different product.
  That still needs a construction-broker conversation. The detail is in the ledger under `recheck_2026-11-19`.
