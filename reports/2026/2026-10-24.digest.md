*What died, in plain English.*
- Driverless delivery vans for the US, which bans Chinese driving software: US companies like DoorDash's partner Also, Nuro and Serve are already funded for it.
- Checking Medicaid recipients' work and income from bank data: big government contractors are already offering states this, some for free.
- AI that checks law firms' bills for insurers: Wolters Kluwer and several newer firms already do it.
- AI for companies fighting their lenders in a debt restructuring: Octus already serves that side.
- Machine-failure warnings for nuclear plants: too few plants, and they already have monitoring.
- AI road maps sold directly to state transport departments: Mach9 already sells to them.

*Lesson.* A rule that keeps a foreign leader out is only an opening if no home-grown company has already raised money for the same job.
