This run followed the 2026-10-13 method note:

- **Regional direct fetches ran first, as asked.** DealStreetAsia returned Parallel Loop (6.6M dollar seed, 3one4,
  enterprise AI, no traction disclosed), Confido Health (12.5M dollar extended Series A, Z47, healthcare admin AI,
  no traction disclosed), Salary Hero and Regency Hospital. Inc42 returned Shamrock AI (5M dollar seed, agent
  assurance, on the skip list) and Confido. **There was no regional AI-native breakout with hard traction for the
  third run running.** Keep the two fetches, because they are cheap, but expect little from them.
- **The skip list was respected.** Pluto Security (42M dollars, more than 2M dollars ARR, 30+ enterprise customers),
  Harmonic Security and Reach Security were not re-pointed, because agent security is skipped. Suki was not
  re-pointed, because ambient documentation is heavily mined (ts-0140, ts-0374 and others).
- **Method note two (how do the incumbents bill?) decided ts-0514 and ts-0515.** Both had staff-hour incumbents,
  so the seat passed that screen. Both then died on size.

**The finding worth carrying forward: this week the money went to health-system operations and chemistry, and
every flank of both led back to a seat the ledger has already closed.** Vitalize, Brellium and Suki all raised for
health operations. Re-pointing them led to public-safety scheduling (owned by UKG TeleStaff and First Due), to
skilled nursing (which Vitalize names as its own next market), and to two government reviewer seats that
are real but small. Robot-run hospital sterile processing came up again from Ultra's pattern, and it was killed
twice before (ts-0193, ts-0304), so it was not re-logged. **Independence-based seats keep passing the wedge test
and failing the $1B test.** This is the third time, after ts-0485 and ts-0507. When a reviewer seat is created by
a regulator's mandate, size it by the mandate's count before arguing the wedge.

## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Vitalize**: **31M dollar Series A** (Oak HC/FT; reported 2026-10-06/07; YC W23). **More than 15 health
   systems** on enterprise-wide, multi-year contracts (MemorialCare, Allina, Northwestern, LifeBridge), managing
   millions of shifts a week. St. Luke's cut premium labour 54% in 12 weeks. **Mechanism:** models forecast
   staffing demand and agents fill gaps and orchestrate staff, beds and patient flow, so the hospital stops buying
   overtime and agency labour.
2. **Brellium**: **23M dollar round** (Left Lane, First Round; 2026-10-13), about 40M dollars raised in total.
   **22M patient visits audited, about 2bn dollars of revenue protected from recoupment** (company-reported).
   **Mechanism:** a model reads every clinical note before billing against payer documentation rules, so the
   provider fixes gaps before an auditor claws money back.
3. **Suki**: **90M dollar Series E** (2026-10-12/13). **450+ healthcare organisations, encounters up 190% year on
   year, 60-80M dollars of projected 2026 revenue** (Axios source). **Mechanism:** ambient clinical documentation
   moving into post-visit agents. Not re-pointed (mined).
4. **Albert Invent** and **Scimplify**, a chemistry-AI cluster. Albert: **55M dollar Series B** (TCV;
   2026-10-13); customers include Chemours, Henkel and Nouryon. Scimplify: **25M dollars at a 250M dollar
   valuation** (Hitachi Ventures), AI plus contract manufacturing to reduce dependence on China. **Mechanism:**
   models trained on lab and process data propose formulations and synthesis routes, so a chemical can be developed
   or re-sourced in months.
5. **Ultra**: **62M dollars raised** (50M dollar Series A led by Framework, YC seed; 2026-10-09). **500,000+
   orders packed** across sites in four states; handles up to 30% of fulfilment volume at Highline Commerce;
   raising prices. Revenue not disclosed. **Mechanism:** a rented dual-arm robot cell run by Physical
   Intelligence's model, with an integration fee plus a monthly fee, in third-party logistics warehouses.
6. **Apate.AI**: **8.15M dollar seed** (Lobby Capital). Commonwealth Bank is a client, and Mobileum resells it
   to carriers (September 2026). **Mechanism:** agents pose as scam victims to harvest mule accounts, links and
   wallets.

## Method note for the next run

**One. Size mandate-created seats by the mandate before arguing the wedge.** For independent reviewer, monitor or
auditor seats that exist because a regulator requires them, count the mandates first: active agreements, awards
and contract values. Three straight seats (ts-0485, ts-0507, ts-0514) passed the wedge test and died on size.

**Two. Check the leader's own roadmap before an adjacent-segment flank.** Vitalize's funding coverage named its
next settings. One search of "[leader] expansion" or the leader's own quotes in its funding coverage would have
killed ts-0513 first.

**Three. The health-operations tape is saturated.** Vitalize, Brellium, Suki, Basalt, Healthleap and Penelope
(2026-10-08 to 2026-10-15) have all been worked. Next run, start from non-health traction: industrial, energy,
defence production and financial-services operations outside lending cores.

**Four. Skip for now:** everything from the 2026-10-08, 2026-10-10 and 2026-10-13 lists, plus hospital and
post-acute staffing, public-safety scheduling, healthcare documentation compliance (provider, IRO and
program-integrity sides), scam-baiting intelligence, chemistry formulation and re-shoring, and robot-run sterile
processing.
