   effort controls, 3× cheaper fast mode), Gemini 3.5 ("agentic Gemini era"),
   GPT-5.5 + real-time audio/translation agents. The whole industry has
   converged on autonomy. *(AI/capability signal)*
3. **SBA SOP 50-10 8 — effective 2025-06-01.** Rewrote change-of-ownership
   financing: 10% equity injection reinstated; a seller note counts toward
   that injection only if on **full standby for the entire 10-year loan term**,
   and only up to 50% of the injection; collateral now required on loans
   ≥ $50k (was $500k). Trade commentary: "many smaller-market M&A deals
   falling through… the days of nearly zero-down acquisitions are gone."
   *(structural / regulatory signal)*
4. **The $5T baby-boomer business transfer.** McKinsey: ~6M SMBs change hands
   by 2035, up to $5T of value; today **92% of exits are closures, only 5%
   sales.** Search-fund / ETA volume rising; PE has rolled up 900+ wealth-mgmt
   RIAs (20 deals in Jan 2026 alone). *(structural / demographic signal)*

## Surviving idea

### ts-0001 — Buy-side SBA capital-stack structuring engine for self-funded acquirers

**The cross (needs both halves, collapses without either):**
- *AI half:* frontier LLM reasoning over the dense, freshly-rewritten SOP 50-10 8
  rules + a buyer's messy deal inputs, producing an optimized, compliant
  capital stack and the seller-facing negotiation collateral. Two years ago this
  was a $5k+ human-advisor task.
- *Structural half:* SOP 50-10 8 (2025-06-01) turned deal structuring into an
  acute, deal-killing problem precisely as the $5T boomer transfer floods the
  lower-middle market with exactly these sub-$5M acquisitions. Without the rule
  change there's no crisis (old rules were lenient/near-zero-down); without
  modern AI it's a retainer.

**The exact buyer.** A first-time **self-funded searcher / ETA buyer** under a
signed LOI on a **$400k–$2M SDE** owner-operated service business (HVAC,
plumbing, landscaping, pest control) — at the financing-structuring stage, not
the browsing stage.

**The trigger moment.** Their SBA lender tells them the seller note they assumed
would cover most of the down payment must be on **full standby for the entire
10-year loan term** to count toward the 10% equity injection — and the
60-something seller, who expected to be paid, balks. The LOI/diligence clock is
running and the deal is days from collapsing over structure, not price.

**Why incumbents structurally can't/won't serve this.**
- *Lender-side software* (Aloan, LenderAI, Biz2x, iBusiness) organizes and
  underwrites the file **to protect the bank, after the structure is fixed** —
  wrong side, wrong incentive.
- *Retained buy-side advisors* (e.g. Munera) "pay for themselves in 18–24
  months" — uneconomic and too slow for a sub-$2M deal.
- *ETA content/communities* (ClearlyAcquired, EBIT, ProjectionHub) educate but
  hand you a blog post, not a compliant structure + a one-page seller
  explainer + a lender-ready term sheet.
- *Brokers* represent the seller. Nobody arms the small self-funded buyer.

**Why now.** SOP 50-10 8 is datable to 2025-06-01 and is *actively killing
deals right now*; the boomer-transfer volume surge is simultaneous. This is
urgent in 2026 in a way it simply was not in 2024.

**Scores (rubric):** Novelty 4 · Why-now 5 · Market/venture-scale 3 ·
Solo-buildable 4 · YC-fit 4 · Founder-fit 4 → **composite 4.0**.

**Honest caveats.** Composite sits *exactly* on the 4.0 line, not clearly above
it. The load-bearing risk is **market/venture-scale (3)**: the wedge buyer is
episodic and low-LTV (buys one business, maybe once a decade). The venture story
requires expanding from the structuring wedge into the transaction/financing
layer — lender-match take-rate, post-close portfolio SaaS, moving up-market to
independent sponsors and PE tuck-ins. Founder-fit is strong (decision-support,
B2B/prosumer workflow, reachable ETA-community GTM, leverages strategy/finance);
no technical-cofounder blocker. Full brief: `ideas/briefs/0001-sba-buyside-deal-structuring.md`.
