# Trend Scout — Report 2026-09-29

**Run type:** scheduled research pass (forty-sixth run under the venture-scale-gated thesis).
**Outcome:** **Zero surviving ideas. 9 new kills (ts-0458 to ts-0466). One held entry updated: ts-0239,
firm water underwriting, is now `watching`.** No brief this run.

This is the third zero in a row. The scan was thinner than the last two. It was a quieter week for
large AI-native rounds, and several of the biggest deals (Tabby, SiMa.ai, Solcoa, CargoX) are
hardware, lending or chips, which fail the AI-native gate before anything can be re-pointed. The run
followed the 2026-09-26 method note:

- **A category's age was checked before its flanks were worked.** The note predicted old categories
  would have their flanks already settled, and it held three times. EU fisheries electronic monitoring
  (ts-0463) has had incumbents since 2012. Insurer investment accounting (ts-0466) is Clearwater's
  whole business. The London market (ts-0458) already had Marsh, Artificial and Ki.
- **Supply-side and new-job openings were chased first.** The one genuinely new job this week is
  **Rainmaker's: verified manufactured precipitation sold per unit to water agencies.** Nothing like it
  existed before 2026. The ledger had no prior entries for cloud seeding, aquifer recharge or SGMA.
  Two "who must sign for or supply this" flanks were opened: independent attribution (ts-0461) and
  recharge for basins cloud seeding cannot reach (ts-0462). Both died, one on venture scale and one on
  the AI-native gate. But the pattern gave real new evidence to a held idea (ts-0239); see below.
- **Counterparty search came first.** It killed ts-0459 (AI-native agency) on the first search.

**The finding worth carrying forward: this week's new-job category (water) is real, but its
AI-native share is thin.** Rainmaker is a physics and hardware company. The AI in it is forecasting
and radar verification. Every flank I could open around it was either a public-agency seat or a
classic modelling job, not a frontier-model one. That is fine for Rainmaker, but for this ledger it
means **water's AI-native opening is on the transaction side, not on the supply side**. It lies in
reading rights, decrees and transfer records at corpus scale, which is exactly what ts-0239 already
claims. Don't open new water-supply flanks; revisit ts-0239 instead.

## Breakout startups scanned

1. **Outmarket**: **34.5M dollar Series B led by SignalFire, 2026-09-28**, at a 335M dollar valuation,
   **four months after its 17M dollar Series A.** Traction: **10,000+ active users, 300+ agencies, more
   than 25% of the Top 100 US agencies.** Next it enters the carrier market. **Mechanism:** agents
   connected to the agency management system do the brokerage's service work (renewals, remarketing,
   submissions, data re-entry). Models can now read carrier documents and legacy system data reliably
   enough to replace this work.

2. **Quartermaster**: **140M dollars (100M Series B led by Insight Partners plus 40M debt),
   2026-09-28**, four months after a 43M dollar Series A. Traction: **SmartMasts on 650+ working vessels
   in 25 countries, 46M sq km covered.** **Mechanism:** rugged camera and radio sensors ride on ships
   that are already at sea, and edge AI turns them into a persistent ocean-surveillance network sold to
   governments, shippers and insurers. Dark fleets and cable sabotage created the demand.

3. **Rainmaker**: **100M dollar Series B, announced 2026-09-21** (DCVC, Lowercarbon, Upfront). Traction:
   **145M+ verified gallons in four months for Oregon and Utah**, and first private proof of manmade
   precipitation. **Mechanism:** drones seed winter storm clouds in forecast-picked windows, and radar
   measures the result. This closes the target-seed-measure loop that killed cloud seeding as a
   purchasable supply in the 1980s.

4. **Ridgeline**: **250M dollar Series E at 1.425bn dollars, 2026-09-16.** Traction: **750bn dollars+ of
   assets committed.** **Mechanism:** a clean-sheet unified front, middle and back office for asset
   managers, with AI running the routine operations work.

5. **Complaion**: **13.5M euro seed led by Eurazeo, 2026-09-28.** Traction: **900+ companies certified;
   about 80% of the process automated.** **Mechanism:** agents prepare European SMEs for ISO and AI Act
   certification.

6. **Wint**: **36M dollar Series D, 2026-09-11.** Traction: **30,000+ systems sold, ~600 enterprises (up
   40%), 1,300+ water-damage incidents prevented.** **Mechanism:** AI on flow data catches leaks
   before they become insurance claims.

## Held book

**ts-0239 (firm water underwriting), status `new` → `watching`.** Rainmaker's round is the first hard
evidence that Western water agencies will pay **per verified unit** for new wet water. Verification,
not supply, is what unlocked the purchase. That is the premise ts-0239 rests on for agricultural
transfers, where satellite ET does the verifying. Rainmaker is a potential *supplier* to a firm-water
book, not a competitor. It sells variable, weather-dependent volume in upstream basins and does not
guarantee a fixed volume to a downstream data centre or fab. Its scores are still rubric v2 (composite
4.7); **re-score under v3 on the next revisit** before comparing it with v3 entries. The open question
for that revisit is Reclamation's Record of Decision on the post-2026 Colorado River operating rules.
Once it is published, check whether it creates or restricts a market in conserved or augmented water.

No change to ts-0424, ts-0410 or the other held entries.

## Method note for the next run

**One. Three zeros in a row means the funding tape is now mostly mined ground.** The weekly large-round
lists keep surfacing categories this ledger has already worked, or hardware that fails the AI-native
gate. Next run, **source from the long tail**: seed and Series A rounds of 3-20M dollars with disclosed
revenue or usage, Show HN and Product Hunt launches with paying users, and YC S26 companies with
revenue numbers that have not been used yet. Megarounds are where the leaders are. Early rounds are
where the new jobs first appear.

**Two. For hardware or physics breakouts (Rainmaker, Quartermaster, Solcoa), go straight to the
transaction layer.** The AI-native opening is almost never in the physical operation. It is in
underwriting, contracting, permitting or trading whatever the physical operation produces. ts-0239 is
the template.

**Three. Skip for now:** insurance distribution (all flanks), maritime intelligence and fisheries
monitoring, weather-modification verification, insurer investment accounting, and US ISO/AI-Act
compliance automation.
