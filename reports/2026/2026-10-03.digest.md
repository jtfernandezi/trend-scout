*Trend Scout — 2026-10-03*

*0 survivors, 8 kills.* No idea cleared all three gates this run.

*What we tried.* We looked at this week's winners: Armadin (AI hackers, 2.5bn dollars), Halluminate (profitable,
paid by four of the top five AI labs), Chamelio and Arceus (legal AI), Kahua (megaproject software, 1bn dollars),
Bretton AI (anti-money-laundering agents), Elio Mortgage and Inbolt. Most ideas tried to be the independent checker
that each winner is barred from being because of its own customers.

*What died, in plain English.*
- Checking mortgage bundles before they're sold to investors: the existing review firms are adding AI themselves.
- AI "mystery shopper" calls to test Medicaid doctor wait times: already sold, and too small.
- The bank's engineer watching data-centre construction loans: engineering firms hold the seat.
- Independent scoring of AI models for banks: Vals AI already does it.
- Auditing banks' money-laundering controls: incumbents plus AI tools.
- AI for judges: Clio bought the leader this week.
- AI hacking of power plants and factories: Frenos.
- Robot vision for small machine shops: Path and GrayMatter.

*Lesson.* An independent checker seat only protects a startup if the conflict rule is actually enforced and the
firms already in the seat bill by the hour. Today's seats failed one or the other.
