*Trend Scout — 2026-10-06*

*0 survivors, 7 kills.* No idea cleared all three gates this run.

*What we tried.* This week we looked mostly at European winners: Vocca (AI receptionist for doctors' phones,
revenue up 7x), Pandektes (searchable European law, revenue up 6x), Procuros (software that lets suppliers' and
shops' ordering systems talk), calliora and Parallel (AI that checks hospital bills), Vinci (AI chip-heat
simulation, 1.5bn dollars) and Finovox (forged-document detection).

*What died, in plain English.*
- Helping German health insurers catch hospital overbilling: the insurers already run a shared AI pilot, and it's
  one country.
- An AI nurse line for out-of-hours calls (like NHS 111): Infermedica and Corti already sell it, and the NHS is
  building its own.
- Checking Medicare Advantage medical records for the government: the contract was just awarded, and it's only
  worth tens of millions.
- AI that connects suppliers to big retailers' ordering systems in the US: Orderful already does it.
- A searchable database of US local court rulings: Trellis already has it.
- Fast AI heat simulation for server racks and batteries: PhysicsX (2.4bn dollars) already sells it.
- Proving whether court evidence was faked with AI: GetReal Security already does it.

*Lesson.* When a European startup copies a US idea, the US version of the narrowed idea is usually already
funded. And a government reviewer job only matters if it's big and open for bidding soon.
