*Trend Scout — 2026-08-25*

*2 survivors, 10 kills.*

*1. Capability credit — lend to the Tier-3 shop against what a model can prove it can build (composite 4.5)*
America and Europe have ordered far more weapons than their factories can make, and the choke point is small machine shops that can't afford to ramp because they're paid only on delivery and no bank can tell whether a 40-person shop can really make 12,000 of a complicated part on time — so we read the drawings and the shop's real quality record, decide if it can deliver, and lend against the order.

*2. Origin of record — tax-liability insurance for country of origin (composite 4.3)*
When a company imports a part it has to declare which country the part really came from, and that answer decides whether it pays no tariff or a brutal one — so we trace the part all the way down through its suppliers, write the answer down, and pay the bill ourselves if the government later disagrees, the way title insurance works for a house.

*Why now:* the US refused to extend the USMCA on 1 July, putting trade rules on an annual cycle, and from 1 September customs can no longer discount the penalty for getting origin wrong. Separately, the US defence backlog is heading to ~$900B and European orders doubled while production didn't.

*Notable kills:* the drone BVLOS compliance play (occupied before the FAA rule even publishes), the EU AI Act content-marking rail (Brussels just deferred the rules it depended on to 2027), and a helium play off the Qatari supply shock (the value belongs to whoever owns the reservoir).
