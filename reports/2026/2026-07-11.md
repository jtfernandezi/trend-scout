## Takeaway for next run

Two consecutive droughts is itself the headline: the reliable generators are exhausted for now. The **systemic/independent-truth square** is tapped across reachable fresh markets (07-09), and this run shows **"go earlier than nameable" is also closing** — the funding market now claims fresh squares inside the same two-week window Trend Scout scans. The honest strategic read: crossing *this fortnight's* capability jump with *this fortnight's* structural shift no longer beats the field, because everyone runs that exact play simultaneously with more capital.

So change the *clock*, not just the shape:
1. **Hunt where the structural shift is real but the market is *slow*, not fast.** The squares that stayed open longest for us (ts-0048/0054/0064/0069/0086) were in **regulated/institutional markets with long sales cycles** (insurance CAT-modeling, private-markets liquidity, utility planning) where a fast-funded cohort *cannot* form in two weeks because the buyer moves slowly. Next run, deliberately prefer a slow-clock institutional buyer over a fast-clock developer/consumer one — even at some cost to "freshness."
2. **Stop re-mining the agent-infra corner.** Enterprise-RL, agent assurance/eval, agent identity/security, agent memory-infra and agentic-commerce are now all confirmed saturated *within the scanning window*. Treat the entire "picks-and-shovels for agents" layer as closed for us and force the next survivor out of a **real-economy vertical or a slow institutional buyer**.
3. **Persistent-memory "durable-responsibilities" is still un-cracked — but only test it against a *slow, data-locked* vertical** (a multi-month human-owned responsibility inside a regulated institution where no incumbent is memory-native), not a horizontal or a fast-consumer surface. If the vertical can be named and funded in two weeks, it's already gone.
4. **ts-0032 (defense-autonomy T&E) — no promotion.** This run surfaced DIU/DAWG's **$100M Autonomous Vehicle Orchestrator Prize** and a **$20M "Project G.I." UAS challenge**, but the Orchestrator Prize is the (killed) ts-0055 swarm-C2 square, and there is still no new *TEV&V-specific* solicitation. Keep ts-0032 `new`; promote to `watching` only when a specific autonomy-class T&E solicitation lands.

Zero survivors is the correct, defensible outcome — for the second run running — and it sharpens rather than muddies the thesis. The ledger and digest are updated; no brief this run.
