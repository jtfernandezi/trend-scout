# Scoring Rubric

Score every surviving idea 1-5 on each dimension. Composite = average.

- **Novelty** — survived the adversarial novelty check; how cleanly, and how
  far from existing players' wording/positioning.
- **Why-now** — what specifically changed recently (not "AI is good now")
  that makes this possible/urgent today and not 2 years ago.
- **Market / venture-scale** — could this plausibly be a venture-scale
  outcome, not a lifestyle business.
- **Solo-buildable-during-MBA** — could one person ship a credible MVP
  nights-and-weekends across a 2-year program.
- **YC-fit** — fundable thesis, clear "makes something people want" story,
  aligns with current YC RFS thinking.
- **Founder-fit** — leverages consulting/strategy/product background
  specifically (GTM-heavy, B2B workflow, vertical SaaS, decision-support).
  Score low (not zero) if the idea is strong but needs a technical
  cofounder — note that explicitly in the brief.

## Ledger lifecycle

`new` → `watching` → `promising` → `building` → `killed`

- `new`: just generated, passed novelty check, not yet revisited
- `watching`: seen again or has a developing signal, not yet acted on
- `promising`: composite score consistently high across multiple runs
- `building`: the operator has started building it
- `killed`: failed novelty check, or judged not viable — keep the reason
